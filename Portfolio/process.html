Five stages

What happens during a Jaire Development project.

The scope may change by project, but the core workflow stays organized and transparent.

  1. Discovery

    I learn what the website needs to accomplish, who it needs to reach, which services matter most, and what action visitors should take.

    Goal: A clear plan before code begins.

  2. Design Direction

    I organize the page structure, establish the visual direction, and map how visitors move from the opening message to the final call to action.

    Goal: A professional design that supports the client’s brand.

  3. Build

    I develop the website with responsive layouts, readable content, accessible interactions, and polished behavior across phones, tablets, and desktop screens.

    Goal: A functional website built from the approved plan.

  4. Test and Refine

    I test navigation, buttons, forms, images, spacing, performance, and common screen sizes, then correct issues before launch.

    Goal: A consistent experience before visitors arrive.

  5. Launch and Handoff

    I publish the site, verify the live version, connect the final links, and prepare the project for future maintenance and updates.

    Goal: A reliable website ready to represent the business.
